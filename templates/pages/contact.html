About CourtVision AI

Bringing AI to Indian Commercial Courts

CourtVision AI is an AI-powered legal research platform designed specifically for Indian commercial court practitioners — judges, advocates, court clerks, and legal researchers.

Our platform leverages the latest in large language model technology — Groq's LLaMA 4 Scout — combined with LangGraph orchestration and FAISS vector search to deliver instant, comprehensive analysis of complex legal documents.

What used to take hours of manual reading can now be accomplished in seconds. Upload a judgment, get a structured AI summary, search semantically, and query the document in plain English — or in Hindi and Tamil.

Mission
Democratize access to legal intelligence for every court professional in India.
Vision
A future where every legal professional has an AI research partner available 24/7.
Privacy
Documents stored securely. No data shared with third parties. Your research stays yours.
Open
Built with open-source technologies — Django, LangGraph, FAISS, and Groq API.
Platform Highlights
Full-Document AI Summarization
Unlike tools that truncate to 4000 chars, our map-reduce pipeline processes every single page of your document before generating the summary.
Permanent Translation Cache
Translated content is stored in the database via SHA-256 hash. Never translated twice — instant retrieval on repeat access.
Role-Based Access
Superusers manage the document library. All authenticated users can view, search, and research — a clean separation of responsibilities.
Persistent Research History
Every research query and its complete LLM answer is saved permanently. Search, revisit, and build on past research.
Embedded PDF Viewer
PDF.js renders court documents directly in the browser — page navigation, zoom, no iframe connection issues.
Deployed on AWS EC2
Production-grade deployment with Nginx + Gunicorn on AWS EC2 with swap space for ML model stability.
