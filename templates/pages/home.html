Platform Features

Everything you need for legal research

Built specifically for Indian commercial courts with AI-first architecture.

AI Document Summarization
Full document map-reduce pipeline using LangGraph and Groq LLaMA 4 Scout. Every page summarized — no truncation. Structured legal findings extracted automatically.
Semantic Vector Search
FAISS-powered vector store indexes every chunk of your documents. Ask questions in plain English and get answers grounded in the exact document passages.
Advanced Case Search
Filter by document type, court, filing date, status. Live AJAX search with keyword highlighting. All cases instantly accessible.
Multilingual Support
Full interface support for English, Hindi (हिन्दी), and Tamil (தமிழ்). AI summaries and research answers translated on demand and cached permanently.
Legal Notes
Take notes directly linked to cases. Pin, tag with colors, search, and access them alongside your research. Quick-note panel for instant capture.
Embedded PDF Viewer
View original court documents directly in the browser using PDF.js — page navigation, zoom controls, no downloads required. Alongside AI summary.
